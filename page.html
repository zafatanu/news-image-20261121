COP31: NO DEALCONFIRMEDAP / AFP / REUTERS

Climate summit runs into Saturday with no deal

COP31 missed its Friday close; the plenary suspended at 22:29. The 2nd draft cut 144 paragraphs to 80 with no mention of coal, oil or gas, and dropped the $120bn-a-year adaptation goal. First COP text to admit warming will pass 1.5°C. A 3rd draft is promised for Saturday morning. 92,000 attendees, the largest ever; COP32 goes to Addis Ababa, Nov 2027.

Three Houthi missiles shot down over Riyadh

The Saudi-led coalition intercepted three ballistic missiles aimed at the capital on Saturday — the fourth night of alerts in a row. No casualties were reported. The Houthis threaten ports and a power plant; the coalition struck Hodeidah port on Friday.
